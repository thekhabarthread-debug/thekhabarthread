Grievance Redressal Policy

In compliance with the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021, The Khabar Thread has appointed a Grievance Officer to address complaints and concerns raised by users regarding content published on this website.

Grievance Officer

Name: Akash Sharma
Email: thekhabarthread@gmail.com

What You Can Report

Readers and users may contact the Grievance Officer regarding concerns such as factual inaccuracies, defamatory content, content violating applicable laws, privacy concerns, or any other grievance related to content published on The Khabar Thread.

How to File a Grievance

Please email the Grievance Officer at the address above with a clear description of your complaint, including the relevant article link (if applicable) and any supporting details. We recommend not sharing sensitive personal information over email.

Response Timeline

The Grievance Officer will acknowledge the complaint within 24 hours of receipt and endeavor to resolve it within 15 days, as per the timelines prescribed under the IT Rules, 2021.
